Quick answers

Where is my progress stored? Can I move it to a new phone?

All progress — unlocked levels, stars, hearts, hints, daily-challenge streak, achievements, and settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's own private storage. There is no account, no cloud save, and no server.

That means:

  • Deleting the app deletes your progress permanently.
  • Progress does not transfer between devices or between iOS and Android.
  • Reinstalling, restoring a device from a backup that predates your latest session, or clearing app data on Android will roll progress back or reset it.

We cannot recover local progress for you, because we never receive it.

I reinstalled and lost everything. Can you restore it?

No. Nothing about your play data leaves the device, so there is no copy on our side to restore. If you are reinstalling to fix a crash, please contact us before deleting the app so we can try other steps first.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game is fully playable offline. No account, sign-in, or download is required at any point.

Does the game show ads or ask me to pay?

No. Version 1.0 is free to play, with no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no paid unlocks.

I ran out of hearts. How do they refill?

Hearts refill over time automatically. If you close the game, refilling continues based on your device's clock, and the remaining time is shown on the level screen. Hearts are never sold; if the timer looks wrong, make sure your device date and time are set automatically.

A hint is not showing me anything.

Hints are free. Tap the hint button once the board is idle — if an arrow is still moving or an animation is playing, the tap can be ignored. If the hint button stays unresponsive across several levels, please send us a bug report (template below).

My daily streak reset.

The daily challenge calendar and streak are computed from your device's local date. Missing a day resets the chain, and manually changing the device date, time zone, or clock can also shift the result. We do not keep a server-side record of streaks, so a reset cannot be undone.

Sound or vibration is not working.

  1. Settings → Sound and Settings → Music control audio inside the game.
  2. Check that the device is not in silent mode and the media volume is up.
  3. Settings → Vibration controls haptic feedback. Haptics are only available on devices that support them, and some system battery-saver modes disable them.
  4. On iOS, check Settings → Sounds & Haptics and make sure haptics are not turned off system-wide.

The screen is too dark / too bright.

Open Settings → Dark mode to switch between the light and dark themes. The choice is remembered on the device.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings and choose a language — English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, or Korean. The choice is remembered on the device.

The app crashes or will not start.

Try, in this order:

  1. Force-close the app and reopen it.
  2. Restart the device.
  3. Make sure your OS is up to date (Android 7.0+).
  4. Free up storage — the game needs a small amount of free space for its save file.

Only as a last resort, delete and reinstall. This erases progress (see above), so contact us first if you would rather keep your levels.

Where can I read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service?

  • In the app: Settings → Privacy Policy and Settings → Terms of Service.
  • From the first-launch screen, both documents are linked before you tap ACCEPT.
  • Online: privacy-policy.html and terms-of-service.html on this site.

How do I get a refund?

Version 1.0 contains no purchases, so there is normally nothing to refund. Any payment handled by a store is handled entirely by Apple or Google, and we cannot issue refunds directly:

  • iOS — reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Android — Google Play → Order history → Request a refund

How do I leave a rating or review?

Settings → Rate Us opens the rating prompt, and the store page for the app. Reviews help a lot, and they are also a fine place to report a problem — though email gets a faster reply.

Does the game ask for permissions?

No sensitive permissions are requested — not camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location. The app does not track you across apps or websites.

Reporting a bug

Email support@lightgame.com with as much of this as you can:

App version      : 1.0.0 (build 1)
Device           : e.g. iPhone 14 Pro / Pixel 8
OS version       : e.g. iOS 18.6 / Android 15
Level            : the level number, or "Daily Challenge"
What happened    : what you saw, in one or two sentences
What you expected: what should have happened
Steps to repeat  : 1) ... 2) ... 3) ...
Screenshot/video : attach if possible

If the problem happens on one specific level, please mention whether it happens every attempt or only occasionally — it saves us a round trip.

Feature requests

We read every message. Send ideas to the same address with "Suggestion" in the subject line. We cannot promise a timeline or that a given idea will ship, but player feedback drives what we work on next.

Before you contact us

  • Confirm you are on the latest version.
  • Try the basic steps above: force-close, restart the device, check toggles.
  • Note that we cannot see your save file, your device, or your gameplay — the details in the template above are how we reproduce a problem.

We will never ask for your password, payment details, or account credentials. If you receive such a request claiming to be from us, it is not from us.
